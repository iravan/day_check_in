## Description

**Day Check In** is your personal daily task management companion designed to help you build consistent habits and track your progress effortlessly.

### Key Features:

✨ **Daily Task Management**
- Create and manage your daily tasks with ease
- Mark tasks as complete with a simple tap
- Visual indicators show your progress at a glance

📅 **Calendar View**
- Beautiful calendar interface showing your completion history
- Highlighted days when all tasks are completed
- Track your consistency over time

📊 **Progress Tracking**
- View statistics of your completed days
- History view organized by month
- See your improvement over time

🎨 **Customizable Themes**
- Choose from 9 beautiful color themes
- Personalize your app experience
- iOS-inspired design

🌍 **Multi-Language Support**
- Available in English, Chinese (Simplified & Traditional), Japanese, Korean, and Thai
- Fully localized interface

### Perfect For:
- Building daily habits
- Tracking personal goals
- Staying organized
- Maintaining consistency
- Personal productivity

### Why Day Check In?
Unlike complex task managers, Day Check In focuses on simplicity and daily consistency. With its intuitive calendar interface and visual progress tracking, you'll stay motivated to complete your tasks every day.

Start your journey to better habits today!
